LifeOS Privacy Policy

LifeOS is a personal life-management app used by its developer. This page describes how it handles data.

What the app stores

Your data (for example tasks, finances and journal entries) is stored on your own device in the browser or app. It is not sent to the developer or any third-party server.

Google Drive sync

If you choose to sign in with Google, LifeOS requests the drive.file and drive.appdata scopes. These let the app create and read only its own backup file in your Google Drive, and never your other files. Backups are encrypted on your device with a passphrase before upload. Google sign-in tokens stay in your browser.

Sharing

LifeOS does not sell, share or analyse your data. LifeOS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data

Delete the backup file from your Google Drive, clear the app's site data, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
